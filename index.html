    FAQ

    What does it change?

    Only the container: video and audio are copied into an MPEG-TS stream. The file keeps its .mp4 name.

    Is my video re-encoded?

    No. Every video frame and audio sample stays bit-identical.

    My phone shows 0:00 or plays only sound?

    Normal. Many galleries can't play this stream format. TikTok reads it fine, upload it as usual.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. It is processed in this tab.

    Which files work?

    MP4 or MOV with H.264 video and AAC audio, up to 5 minutes, not fragmented.

    Does it always work?

    No guarantee: TikTok can change its pipeline any time. Upload on desktop web, not in the app.
