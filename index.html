    FAQ

    What does it change?

    Only the MP4 container: moov first, audio/video interleaving, extra sample-table entries, the bitrate box, timestamps and editor metadata.

    Is my video re-encoded?

    No. Every video frame and audio sample stays bit-identical.

    Is my file uploaded anywhere?

    No. It is processed in this tab.

    Which files work?

    MP4 or MOV up to 4 GB, not fragmented.

    Does it always work?

    No guarantee: TikTok can change its pipeline any time. Upload on desktop web, not in the app.
